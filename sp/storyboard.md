# Schuberg Philis — "Some systems never sleep"

40 s · 8 scenes · 150 shapes · 120 BPM · A minor → C major.

**Metaphor.** The hero (shape 0) is the heartbeat of a business: a pulse on a monitor at 3 a.m. It becomes the beacon of a plane,
the "paid" check, the current that lights a city, the one dot that keeps working, and then the **backslash** from
the Schuberg Philis logo, which cuts through the failing grid and floods the screen with the brand blue. It
runs the uptime line, leads the team, and finally slices down to become the backslash in the logo.
